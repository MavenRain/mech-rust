E1: lift `let` and round-trip it through the seed corpus

The lifter accepts `let name: T = v;` and `let name = v;` before the tail
of a block (`KLet`), and a block in an argument position whose first
statement is a let (`KBlock`). A let with a pattern, with no initializer
or with no tail after it is a located refusal; the parser keeps refusing
`let mut`.

Fixtures: refuse crates `12_let_mut` and `13_let_pattern` replace `01_let`;
refuse crate `14_let_no_type` shows that a failed synthesis of an untyped
initializer gives the let refusal, not the CD9 text; seed `10_let` adds
`lightTwice` (a let in the tail) with a RED control that changes the
imported initializer; `let` rows in the infer and lower python gates;
four rows in neg_classify.mech (three ED2 name collisions and one
binder of the non-Copy type `MechNat`).

Docs: `## Let (E1)` in EMIT.md and IMPORT.md, ROUNDTRIP.md known limits.

Preserve let blocks in printed match scrutinees and if conditions instead
of emitting the flat-layout placeholder. Synthesize let expression types
in scrutinees and nested initializers while retaining initializer checks.
Add parser, inference, evaluation and round-trip regressions for these paths.
